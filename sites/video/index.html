Questions

How long can my GIF be?

Up to 60 seconds, but keep it short. GIF is an old format that stores every frame as a full picture, so a 15-second clip at 480 px is often 5 to 20 MB, about ten times the size of the video it came from. Many chat apps and sites cap GIFs somewhere between 8 and 15 MB. For a smaller file, trim it, pick 320 px, or use 10 frames per second.

Why do the colors look a bit grainy?

A GIF can only use 256 colors. vid2gif.lol picks the best 255 for your clip and mixes them in tiny dots to fake the rest, which is what keeps skies and skin from turning into stripes. If your clip is a screen recording with flat colors, pick "Flat" for a cleaner, smaller file.

Will it work on my phone?

Short clips usually do. Long videos and 4K videos can be slow or fail on phones, because the phone has to decode the video and hold the frames in memory. If it stalls or the tab reloads, trim to a few seconds, pick 320 or 480 px, or use a computer. For MP3s, MP4 and MOV files are read in small pieces, so long ones usually work. A WebM's sound has to be decoded in one go, so long WebM files are harder on phones.

Which videos work?

Anything your browser can play: MP4 and MOV from phones and cameras, and WebM from screen recorders. iPhone videos saved as HEVC play in Safari and in recent Chrome and Edge on most computers. Some browsers can't play them, and if your browser can't play a video, this tool can't read it either. Portrait videos come out the right way up.

The MP3 option says there's no audio track.

The video file doesn't contain any sound. That's common with screen recordings made with the microphone off, GIF-style clips from social sites, and some phone videos shared through apps that strip the sound. There's nothing to save, so pick a different video.

Does the GIF keep the sound?

No. GIFs can't hold sound. That's what the MP3 option is for: it saves the sound track on its own, for the whole video or just the part between your start and end times.
